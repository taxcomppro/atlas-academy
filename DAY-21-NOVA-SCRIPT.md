## Narration script

### Scene 1 - Mission hook

Welcome to Day 21. Today your mission is to create staff accountability forms. Make every responsibility visible and acknowledged. This mission turns an important launch decision into a documented operating standard that your office can repeat, review, and improve.

### Scene 2 - Why this mission matters

Document staff duties, required procedures, security expectations, escalation rules, attestations, and consequences.

Staff forms should define what each role must do, what it may not do, when a matter must be escalated, and what evidence proves completion.

Use signed acknowledgments for due diligence, data security, document handling, client communication, review, and policy updates. Pair the forms with training and active supervision.

### Scene 3 - Four operating controls

First, focus on role. Assigned duties, authority, limits, and ownership. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

Next, focus on standards. Required procedures, security, and service expectations. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

Then, focus on escalation. When to stop, ask, report, or obtain review. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

Finally, focus on attestation. Signature, date, training record, and update history. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

### Scene 4 - Build the action plan

Keep this operating rule in view: A signature does not replace training, supervision, or corrective action. When something is uncertain, pause the affected decision, identify the official source or qualified professional who can resolve it, and preserve the answer with the mission records. A deadline, sales claim, or busy-season shortcut is not evidence.

Now complete the Accountability Form Blueprint. Work through all four mission areas, name the accountable owner, and identify the document, test, approval, or other evidence that will prove each item is ready. Use the action plan to expose gaps early, while they can still be corrected without disrupting a client or filing deadline.

Your professional-review prompt is this: Name the first role you will document and the decision that role is never allowed to make alone. Write the answer in plain language. List the facts that support it, the questions that remain open, and the date or event that will trigger another review. Do not place Social Security numbers, client return information, account credentials, or other sensitive identifiers in the Academy worksheet.

### Scene 5 - Control and completion

Before you complete the checkpoint, review the mission from the client's point of view and the office owner's point of view. Ask whether the process is clear, secure, compliant, financially sustainable, and understandable to another trained team member. If the process depends on one person's memory, convert it into a written step, assigned control, or retained record.

When the action plan and checkpoint are complete, continue to Build Audit-Ready Client Files. Bring the records from this mission forward so the next decision builds on verified work instead of starting over. I will meet you at the next mission.
