# Day 10 - Nova Production Script

**Lesson:** Choose Your Office Model
**Presenter:** Nova Grant, Launch Commander
**Format:** 16:9 landscape, professional and conversational
**Target length:** 3-5 minutes
**Narration:** 433 words; approximately 3.2 minutes at 135 words per minute
**Toolkit source:** pages 35-38

## Narration script

### Scene 1 - Mission hook

Your office model affects every workflow that follows.

### Scene 2 - What to do

A home office can lower overhead and eliminate a commute, but it requires clear separation from household activity, strong privacy controls, secure technology, and confirmation of zoning, insurance, and client-access rules. Virtual and shared environments can provide flexibility without a long lease. The tradeoff is control. You still need a private place for conversations, secure printing and storage, dependable connectivity, professional scheduling, and a consistent client experience.

### Scene 3 - Four operating controls

Use this four-part checklist while you work. Home office. Lower overhead and commute time can help a new office, but privacy, zoning, household separation, signage, and client access require careful planning. Virtual or shared. Flexible space can reduce fixed cost, but document security, private conversations, storage, technology, branding, and scheduling must be controlled. Commercial space. Visibility and dedicated client flow can support growth while adding lease, utility, furnishing, insurance, staffing, and security obligations. Choose for operations. The best model is the one your procedures, budget, service mix, and risk controls can sustain consistently.

### Scene 4 - Build the action plan

Commercial space offers visibility, dedicated workflows, and room to grow, while adding rent, utilities, furnishings, insurance, staffing, security, and lease commitments. Your mission is to compare the realistic options using the full operating cost and the controls your tax office needs. Choose the model you can operate securely and consistently, not simply the model that looks impressive. In your Day 10 action plan, document the following. Models under consideration: Home, virtual, shared, commercial, or hybrid. Privacy and confidential conversations: How each option protects client information. Document and device security: Storage, access, network, printing, and disposal. Client access: Parking, transit, accessibility, appointments, and waiting. True monthly cost: Rent, utilities, technology, insurance, furnishings, staffing. Growth and flexibility: Capacity, branding, hours, and expansion limits.

### Scene 5 - Control and completion

As you complete this mission, use a simple three-part control: source, evidence, and owner. Verify the requirement with an official source or a qualified professional. Preserve the document, confirmation, or decision that supports your work. Then assign the next action and due date to a specific person. Do not place Social Security numbers, client return information, account credentials, or other sensitive identifiers in the Academy planner. Before you submit, purchase, sign, or activate anything, separate confirmed facts from assumptions and open questions. Mark every dependency that could delay the next mission. If an item still requires approval or professional review, record it as pending rather than treating it as complete. Include a practical fallback when a vendor, agency, location, or document is not available on schedule. Finish by saving your action plan, recording every professional question, opening both lesson resources, and completing the checkpoint.

## HeyGen script framing

This script is a concept and theme to convey - not a verbatim transcript. You have full creative freedom to expand, elaborate, add examples, and fill the duration naturally. Do not pad with silence or pauses.
