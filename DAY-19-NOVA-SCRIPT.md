**Presenter:** Nova Grant, Launch Commander

## Narration script

### Scene 1 - Mission hook

Welcome to Day 19. Today your mission is to build your compliance infrastructure. Turn professional standards into daily operating controls. This mission turns an important launch decision into a documented operating standard that your office can repeat, review, and improve.

### Scene 2 - Why this mission matters

Create written policies, evidence requirements, review checkpoints, training, and oversight that support defensible tax preparation.

Due diligence requires knowledge of the law, reasonable inquiries, and records that support the conclusions reached on a return.

Translate those duties into written procedures for intake, identity, eligibility, documentation, preparation, review, signatures, data security, retention, escalation, and staff accountability.

### Scene 3 - Four operating controls

First, focus on policies. Written standards for recurring risks and responsibilities. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

Next, focus on evidence. Required documents, notes, forms, and retention. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

Then, focus on review. Checkpoints, escalation, corrections, and signoff. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

Finally, focus on oversight. Training, monitoring, accountability, and updates. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

### Scene 4 - Build the action plan

Keep this operating rule in view: A policy that is not trained, used, reviewed, and evidenced is not an operating control. When something is uncertain, pause the affected decision, identify the official source or qualified professional who can resolve it, and preserve the answer with the mission records. A deadline, sales claim, or busy-season shortcut is not evidence.

Now complete the Compliance Control Map. Work through all four mission areas, name the accountable owner, and identify the document, test, approval, or other evidence that will prove each item is ready. Use the action plan to expose gaps early, while they can still be corrected without disrupting a client or filing deadline.

Your professional-review prompt is this: Identify the compliance control that must exist before the first client appointment and name its owner. Write the answer in plain language. List the facts that support it, the questions that remain open, and the date or event that will trigger another review. Do not place Social Security numbers, client return information, account credentials, or other sensitive identifiers in the Academy worksheet.

### Scene 5 - Control and completion

Before you complete the checkpoint, review the mission from the client's point of view and the office owner's point of view. Ask whether the process is clear, secure, compliant, financially sustainable, and understandable to another trained team member. If the process depends on one person's memory, convert it into a written step, assigned control, or retained record.

When the action plan and checkpoint are complete, continue to Use Due Diligence Questionnaires. Bring the records from this mission forward so the next decision builds on verified work instead of starting over. I will meet you at the next mission.
