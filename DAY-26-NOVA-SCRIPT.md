## Narration script

### Scene 1 - Mission hook

Welcome to Day 26. Today your mission is to hire the right roles. Assign the work before adding the people. This mission turns an important launch decision into a documented operating standard that your office can repeat, review, and improve.

### Scene 2 - Why this mission matters

Match preparation, review, administration, technology, service, and management duties to accountable role owners.

Start with the work: client intake, preparation, review, administration, technology support, customer service, compliance, and office management.

Separate incompatible duties where possible, define decision authority, document qualifications, use structured interviews, and confirm that staffing capacity matches expected volume and risk.

### Scene 3 - Four operating controls

First, focus on production. Preparers and specialists who complete technical work. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

Next, focus on control. Reviewers and compliance owners who challenge and approve. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

Then, focus on operations. Scheduling, documents, payments, records, and facilities. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

Finally, focus on experience. Client service, follow-up, issue resolution, and communication. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

### Scene 4 - Build the action plan

Keep this operating rule in view: Adding a person without a defined role often adds handoff errors instead of capacity. When something is uncertain, pause the affected decision, identify the official source or qualified professional who can resolve it, and preserve the answer with the mission records. A deadline, sales claim, or busy-season shortcut is not evidence.

Now complete the Role Ownership Matrix. Work through all four mission areas, name the accountable owner, and identify the document, test, approval, or other evidence that will prove each item is ready. Use the action plan to expose gaps early, while they can still be corrected without disrupting a client or filing deadline.

Your professional-review prompt is this: Name the first role you need, the result it owns, and the qualification you will not compromise. Write the answer in plain language. List the facts that support it, the questions that remain open, and the date or event that will trigger another review. Do not place Social Security numbers, client return information, account credentials, or other sensitive identifiers in the Academy worksheet.

### Scene 5 - Control and completion

Before you complete the checkpoint, review the mission from the client's point of view and the office owner's point of view. Ask whether the process is clear, secure, compliant, financially sustainable, and understandable to another trained team member. If the process depends on one person's memory, convert it into a written step, assigned control, or retained record.

When the action plan and checkpoint are complete, continue to Train Your Team. Bring the records from this mission forward so the next decision builds on verified work instead of starting over. I will meet you at the next mission.
