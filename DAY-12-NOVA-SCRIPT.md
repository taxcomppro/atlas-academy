# Day 12 - Nova Production Script

**Lesson:** Negotiate Your Lease
**Presenter:** Nova Grant, Launch Commander
**Format:** 16:9 landscape, professional and conversational
**Target length:** 3-5 minutes
**Narration:** 425 words; approximately 3.1 minutes at 135 words per minute
**Toolkit source:** pages 42-45

## Narration script

### Scene 1 - Mission hook

A lease can shape your operating cost and flexibility for years, so treat it as a business system rather than a simple rent quote.

### Scene 2 - What to do

Calculate the full obligation. Include base rent, common-area charges, utilities, taxes, insurance, parking, security, maintenance, required improvements, and restoration costs. Confirm that the permitted use supports tax preparation, client appointments, staffing, hours, signs, privacy improvements, secure technology, and any complementary services you plan to offer. Clarify who pays for repairs, accessibility work, build-out, damage, and compliance requirements.

### Scene 3 - Four operating controls

Use this four-part checklist while you work. Price the entire obligation. Base rent may not include common-area charges, utilities, taxes, insurance, repairs, security, parking, or required improvements. Confirm permitted use. The agreement should support tax preparation, appointments, staffing, signage, hours, privacy modifications, and the intended technology. Allocate responsibilities. Clarify maintenance, repairs, build-out, accessibility, compliance, damage, restoration, and insurance responsibilities. Plan the exit. Review term, renewal, increases, assignment, sublease, default, personal guarantees, early termination, and restoration obligations with counsel.

### Scene 4 - Build the action plan

Then examine the future. Review renewal options, increases, notice periods, assignment, sublease, default, personal guarantees, early termination, and the condition in which the space must be returned. Your mission is to prepare a structured lease review for legal and financial guidance. Do not sign because the space feels right; sign only after the obligations and exit risks are understood. In your Day 12 action plan, document the following. Term and renewal: Start, end, options, notice, and increases. Total occupancy cost: Rent, common charges, utilities, taxes, insurance, parking. Permitted use and hours: Tax preparation, staffing, appointments, events, access. Improvements and maintenance: Build-out, repairs, accessibility, restoration. Signage and visibility: Exterior, window, monument, and directory rights. Exit and risk terms: Guarantee, default, assignment, sublease, termination.

### Scene 5 - Control and completion

As you complete this mission, use a simple three-part control: source, evidence, and owner. Verify the requirement with an official source or a qualified professional. Preserve the document, confirmation, or decision that supports your work. Then assign the next action and due date to a specific person. Do not place Social Security numbers, client return information, account credentials, or other sensitive identifiers in the Academy planner. Before you submit, purchase, sign, or activate anything, separate confirmed facts from assumptions and open questions. Mark every dependency that could delay the next mission. If an item still requires approval or professional review, record it as pending rather than treating it as complete. Include a practical fallback when a vendor, agency, location, or document is not available on schedule. Finish by saving your action plan, recording every professional question, opening both lesson resources, and completing the checkpoint.
