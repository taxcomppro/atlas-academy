# Day 14 - Nova Production Script

**Lesson:** Select Office Supplies and Technology
**Presenter:** Nova Grant, Launch Commander
**Format:** 16:9 landscape, professional and conversational
**Target length:** 3-5 minutes
**Narration:** 420 words; approximately 3.1 minutes at 135 words per minute
**Toolkit source:** pages 47-49

## Narration script

### Scene 1 - Mission hook

Office technology should support a secure tax workflow from intake through final delivery.

### Scene 2 - What to do

Map the work before shopping. Identify where documents arrive, how they are scanned, who prepares and reviews returns, how signatures and payments are handled, and where records are stored or securely destroyed. Select supported computers, sufficient monitors, reliable scanning and printing, secure networking, appropriate phones, and protected storage. Include access controls, encryption, backups, updates, screen privacy, surge protection, and a plan for equipment failure.

### Scene 3 - Four operating controls

Use this four-part checklist while you work. Design from workflow. Map intake, scanning, preparation, review, signature, payment, delivery, storage, and disposal before purchasing equipment. Prioritize security. Use supported devices, access controls, secure networking, encryption, backups, updates, screen privacy, and appropriate document disposal. Buy for reliability. Evaluate warranty, support, replacement options, compatibility, performance, and downtime risk rather than price alone. Control consumables. Standardize paper, toner, folders, labels, mailing, receipts, cleaning, and emergency supplies with reorder levels.

### Scene 4 - Build the action plan

Then standardize the physical supplies: paper, toner, folders, labels, receipts, mailing materials, cleaning items, and emergency stock. Establish reorder levels so the office does not discover a shortage during peak production. Your mission is to build the minimum secure equipment plan and separate launch essentials from upgrades that can wait. Reliability, security, and workflow fit matter more than appearance alone. In your Day 14 action plan, document the following. Workstations and monitors: Users, specifications, support, and replacement. Scanning and printing: Volume, duplex, secure release, maintenance, supplies. Network and communication: Internet, firewall, Wi-Fi, phone, video meetings. Security and continuity: Encryption, backups, updates, access, surge protection. Furniture and privacy: Desks, seating, locks, screens, sound, client flow. Consumables and reorder levels: Paper, toner, folders, labels, mailing, cleaning.

### Scene 5 - Control and completion

As you complete this mission, use a simple three-part control: source, evidence, and owner. Verify the requirement with an official source or a qualified professional. Preserve the document, confirmation, or decision that supports your work. Then assign the next action and due date to a specific person. Do not place Social Security numbers, client return information, account credentials, or other sensitive identifiers in the Academy planner. Before you submit, purchase, sign, or activate anything, separate confirmed facts from assumptions and open questions. Mark every dependency that could delay the next mission. If an item still requires approval or professional review, record it as pending rather than treating it as complete. Include a practical fallback when a vendor, agency, location, or document is not available on schedule. Finish by saving your action plan, recording every professional question, opening both lesson resources, and completing the checkpoint.

## HeyGen script framing

This script is a concept and theme to convey - not a verbatim transcript. You have full creative freedom to expand, elaborate, add examples, and fill the duration naturally. Do not pad with silence or pauses.

## Production direction

Use the selected Nova presenter with a confident, practical delivery. Keep the Atlas Academy navy, blue, white, and gold palette. Use clean motion graphics for checklists and process steps, plus restrained stock office footage only when it improves understanding. Do not display sensitive identification numbers, tax return data, account credentials, or invented government forms.
