# Day 29 - Nova Production Script

**Lesson:** Complete a Mock Client Session
**Presenter:** Nova Grant, Launch Commander
**Format:** 16:9 landscape, professional and conversational
**Target length:** 3-5 minutes
**Narration:** 574 words; approximately 4.3 minutes at 135 words per minute
**Toolkit source:** page 75

## Narration script

### Scene 1 - Mission hook

Welcome to Day 29. Today your mission is to complete a mock client session. Test the experience before a real client depends on it. This mission turns an important launch decision into a documented operating standard that your office can repeat, review, and improve.

### Scene 2 - Why this mission matters

Run a complete simulated appointment through intake, documents, preparation, review, communication, payment, filing readiness, and closeout.

Use a realistic scenario and move it through every system exactly as a live file would move. Time the appointment, observe handoffs, test permissions, inspect messages, and require the same evidence and review.

Debrief immediately. Record delays, confusion, missing forms, duplicate work, control failures, and client-experience issues. Assign corrections and rerun any critical failure before opening.

### Scene 3 - Four operating controls

First, focus on arrival. Scheduling, welcome, engagement, identity, and intake. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

Next, focus on production. Documents, questions, preparation, status, and handoffs. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

Then, focus on control. Review, corrections, approval, security, and signatures. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

Finally, focus on closeout. Payment, filing readiness, delivery, retention, and follow-up. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

### Scene 4 - Build the action plan

Keep this operating rule in view: Do not coach around a broken step during the test. Record the failure so the process can be corrected. When something is uncertain, pause the affected decision, identify the official source or qualified professional who can resolve it, and preserve the answer with the mission records. A deadline, sales claim, or busy-season shortcut is not evidence.

Now complete the Mock Session Debrief. Work through all four mission areas, name the accountable owner, and identify the document, test, approval, or other evidence that will prove each item is ready. Use the action plan to expose gaps early, while they can still be corrected without disrupting a client or filing deadline.

Your professional-review prompt is this: Record the most serious failure found in the mock session, its root cause, owner, and retest date. Write the answer in plain language. List the facts that support it, the questions that remain open, and the date or event that will trigger another review. Do not place Social Security numbers, client return information, account credentials, or other sensitive identifiers in the Academy worksheet.

### Scene 5 - Control and completion

Before you complete the checkpoint, review the mission from the client's point of view and the office owner's point of view. Ask whether the process is clear, secure, compliant, financially sustainable, and understandable to another trained team member. If the process depends on one person's memory, convert it into a written step, assigned control, or retained record.

When the action plan and checkpoint are complete, continue to Open Your Tax Office. Bring the records from this mission forward so the next decision builds on verified work instead of starting over. I will meet you at the next mission.

## HeyGen script framing

This script is a concept and theme to convey - not a verbatim transcript. You have full creative freedom to expand, elaborate, add examples, and fill the duration naturally. Do not pad with silence or pauses.
