TOOLKIT LESSON

Your entity is one layer of protection

An LLC or corporation can create legal separation between personal finances and business obligations when it is properly formed, maintained, and operated. That separation does not erase professional responsibility for negligence, fraud, due-diligence failures, or other noncompliant conduct.

Tax treatment must match the operating reality.

Projected profit, owner compensation, payroll readiness, state treatment, and administrative capacity all matter. No election should be made solely because someone advertised a tax-saving shortcut.

PROTECTION AND TAX PROFILE

Know what each choice changes - and what it does not

SOLE PROPRIETOR

Direct owner reporting

Business activity is generally reported by the owner. There is no separate legal entity, and net earnings may be subject to self-employment tax.

LLC

Legal form with tax flexibility

The underlying legal entity may provide separation. Federal tax classification depends on ownership and any valid elections.

C CORPORATION

Separate taxpayer

The corporation generally pays tax at the entity level. Distributions to shareholders can create an additional tax layer.

S CORPORATION ELECTION

Pass-through with payroll discipline

Eligible items generally pass through to shareholders. Shareholder-employees who perform services may require reasonable compensation and payroll compliance.

Insurance remains essential.

Professional liability or errors-and-omissions coverage can add protection for preparation errors, missed deadlines, and professional disputes. Coverage terms and exclusions must be reviewed carefully.
