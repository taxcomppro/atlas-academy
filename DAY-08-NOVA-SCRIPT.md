## Narration script

### Scene 1 - Mission hook

Phase Two begins with the individual credential used by paid federal tax return preparers: the PTIN.

### Scene 2 - What to do

The PTIN belongs to the individual, not the tax office. Anyone who prepares or substantially assists in preparing federal tax returns for compensation should verify the current IRS requirement and maintain an active PTIN when required. Use the official IRS Tax Professional PTIN system. Prepare the information requested by the current application, which may include identity details, prior-year return information, professional credentials, and other disclosures. Enter that information only in the secure official process, not in this Academy worksheet.

### Scene 3 - Four operating controls

Use this four-part checklist while you work. Know who needs one. An individual who prepares or assists in preparing federal tax returns for compensation generally needs an active PTIN. Use the IRS system. Create or access the official IRS Tax Professional PTIN account and follow the current application instructions. Prepare accurate information. Have personal identifying information, prior-year return information when requested, and professional details ready. Renew annually. Track renewal timing and retain the application, confirmation, payment, and correspondence records.

### Scene 4 - Build the action plan

After submission, preserve the confirmation, payment record, and correspondence. PTINs require annual renewal, so assign the renewal to a calendar owner rather than relying on memory. Your mission is to prepare the application packet, document the status, and establish the renewal control. The credential is personal, but the office needs a system that verifies every paid preparer remains active. In your Day 8 action plan, document the following. Applicant name: Individual preparer. Official IRS account status: Create, access, or recover. Information packet: Identity, prior-year return, professional details. Application status: Not started, in progress, submitted, or active. Confirmation storage: Secure record location. Annual renewal control: Calendar owner and reminder timing.

### Scene 5 - Control and completion

As you complete this mission, use a simple three-part control: source, evidence, and owner. Verify the requirement with an official source or a qualified professional. Preserve the document, confirmation, or decision that supports your work. Then assign the next action and due date to a specific person. Do not place Social Security numbers, client return information, account credentials, or other sensitive identifiers in the Academy planner. Before you submit, purchase, sign, or activate anything, separate confirmed facts from assumptions and open questions. Mark every dependency that could delay the next mission. If an item still requires approval or professional review, record it as pending rather than treating it as complete. Include a practical fallback when a vendor, agency, location, or document is not available on schedule. Finish by saving your action plan, recording every professional question, opening both lesson resources, and completing the checkpoint.
