## Narration script

### Scene 1 - Mission hook

Welcome to Day 27. Today your mission is to train your team. Turn policies into confident, observable performance. This mission turns an important launch decision into a documented operating standard that your office can repeat, review, and improve.

### Scene 2 - Why this mission matters

Deliver technical, operational, security, compliance, and service training with practice, assessment, and documented completion.

A complete program covers tax knowledge, software, intake, documentation, due diligence, security, client communication, escalation, and the office workflow.

Use demonstrations, scenarios, mock files, knowledge checks, coaching, and role certification. Refresh training when laws, software, policies, risks, or responsibilities change.

### Scene 3 - Four operating controls

First, focus on knowledge. Tax, policy, security, software, and service standards. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

Next, focus on practice. Scenarios, demonstrations, mock files, and supervised work. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

Then, focus on proof. Assessments, observations, signoff, and role certification. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

Finally, focus on reinforce. Coaching, updates, quality findings, and recurring refreshers. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

### Scene 4 - Build the action plan

Keep this operating rule in view: A completed slide deck does not prove that a team member can perform the work correctly. When something is uncertain, pause the affected decision, identify the official source or qualified professional who can resolve it, and preserve the answer with the mission records. A deadline, sales claim, or busy-season shortcut is not evidence.

Now complete the Training Readiness Plan. Work through all four mission areas, name the accountable owner, and identify the document, test, approval, or other evidence that will prove each item is ready. Use the action plan to expose gaps early, while they can still be corrected without disrupting a client or filing deadline.

Your professional-review prompt is this: Describe the mock task each role must complete successfully before working with a live client. Write the answer in plain language. List the facts that support it, the questions that remain open, and the date or event that will trigger another review. Do not place Social Security numbers, client return information, account credentials, or other sensitive identifiers in the Academy worksheet.

### Scene 5 - Control and completion

Before you complete the checkpoint, review the mission from the client's point of view and the office owner's point of view. Ask whether the process is clear, secure, compliant, financially sustainable, and understandable to another trained team member. If the process depends on one person's memory, convert it into a written step, assigned control, or retained record.

When the action plan and checkpoint are complete, continue to Run the Grand Opening Checklist. Bring the records from this mission forward so the next decision builds on verified work instead of starting over. I will meet you at the next mission.
