# Atlas Academy - HeyGen Video Agent Upload Guide

Use one `DAY-XX-NOVA-SCRIPT.md` file for each video. Paste the full contents into a new HeyGen Video Agent project.

## Course production settings

- Course: 30 Day Tax Office Launch
- Public instructor name: Nova Grant
- HeyGen stock presenter: Talia
- Preferred look: Talia Office13 P2 A1
- Voice: Radiant Riley - Friendly
- Format: landscape, 16:9
- Duration: 3-5 minutes
- Language: American English
- Tone: warm, encouraging, polished, practical, and professional
- Brand colors: deep navy `#07182D`, electric blue `#159FDB`, white, and restrained gold `#F7C948`

## Production sequence

1. Open a new Video Agent project.
2. Paste one complete daily script.
3. Confirm landscape 16:9 and a 3-5 minute target.
4. Select the Nova/Talia presenter and Radiant Riley voice when HeyGen offers the choice.
5. Review the generated outline, presenter, duration, and on-screen wording before approving the final render.
6. Generate only one lesson at a time so an incorrect presenter or visual direction does not consume credits across a batch.
7. Download the finished MP4 and name it `Day-XX-Nova-[lesson-name].mp4`.
8. Send the downloaded MP4 back to Codex for insertion into the lesson page and Vercel publishing.

## Credit-control rule

Do not approve the final render until the Video Agent draft shows the correct instructor, correct lesson title, 3-5 minute duration, and the required critical on-screen text. Creating the video manually in HeyGen still uses HeyGen credits; the purpose of this workflow is to give you direct control before each render and prevent avoidable regenerations.

## Important content rule

These lessons are educational. Do not allow Video Agent to turn general guidance into individualized legal or tax advice, fabricate government forms, display sensitive identifiers, or promise tax outcomes.
