# Day 7 - Nova Production Script

**Lesson:** Build Your Startup Record File
**Presenter:** Nova Grant, Launch Commander
**Format:** 16:9 landscape, professional and conversational
**Target length:** 3-5 minutes
**Narration:** 424 words; approximately 3.1 minutes at 135 words per minute
**Toolkit source:** pages 25-32

## Narration script

### Scene 1 - Mission hook

Before moving into registrations and office setup, organize the foundation you have built.

### Scene 2 - What to do

Create one permanent record system for legal formation, ownership, EIN and tax notices, banking, insurance, licenses, contracts, and renewals. Use consistent names and preserve the official source document, not just a note saying that a task was completed. For each category, identify where the record is stored, who owns the next action, and whether anything is missing. Sensitive records require restricted access, secure storage, and dependable backups. Do not place confidential business or personal identifiers in an unsecured workspace.

### Scene 3 - Four operating controls

Use this four-part checklist while you work. Use a permanent index. Create consistent folders for legal, tax, banking, ownership, insurance, licenses, contracts, and renewals. Preserve source documents. Keep accepted filings, official notices, signed agreements, and confirmations rather than relying on summaries. Control access. Limit sensitive records to authorized roles and protect digital copies with secure storage and backups. Track obligations. Add annual reports, licenses, elections, renewals, and other recurring requirements to a compliance calendar.

### Scene 4 - Build the action plan

Then convert recurring obligations into calendar controls. Annual reports, licenses, elections, insurance renewals, and similar requirements should have an owner, a due date, and a review checkpoint. Your mission is to build the record index and close the gaps from Days One through Six. A well-organized startup file makes every later registration, audit response, and vendor relationship easier. In your Day 7 action plan, document the following. Legal formation records: Location and missing items. Ownership and governance: Signed agreements, resolutions, amendments. Federal and state tax records: EIN, elections, registrations, notices. Banking and financial controls: Account records, authorizations, policies. Insurance, licenses, and contracts: Coverage, permits, vendors, leases. Renewal calendar: Responsible owner and next due dates.

### Scene 5 - Control and completion

As you complete this mission, use a simple three-part control: source, evidence, and owner. Verify the requirement with an official source or a qualified professional. Preserve the document, confirmation, or decision that supports your work. Then assign the next action and due date to a specific person. Do not place Social Security numbers, client return information, account credentials, or other sensitive identifiers in the Academy planner. Before you submit, purchase, sign, or activate anything, separate confirmed facts from assumptions and open questions. Mark every dependency that could delay the next mission. If an item still requires approval or professional review, record it as pending rather than treating it as complete. Include a practical fallback when a vendor, agency, location, or document is not available on schedule. Finish by saving your action plan, recording every professional question, opening both lesson resources, and completing the checkpoint.
