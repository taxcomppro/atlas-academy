NOVA'S OPENING BRIEFING

Build the office as a system

Welcome to the 30 Day Tax Office Launch. Over the next 30 missions, we are not merely choosing a name or arranging a room. We are building a tax office that can operate with structure, consistency, professionalism, and documented compliance.

The toolkit explains that technical knowledge alone is not enough. A sustainable office also needs the right entity, registrations, banking, workspace, vendors, client workflow, staff expectations, documentation standards, and opening-day controls. Each mission will convert one part of that toolkit into a decision or completed action.
