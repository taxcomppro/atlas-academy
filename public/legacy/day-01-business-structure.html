TOOLKIT LESSON

Your structure is an operating decision

Your structure influences personal liability, tax treatment, ownership control, the ability to add partners or investors, credibility with financial institutions, and the administrative systems your office must maintain.

01Liability protection
02Tax treatment
03Ownership control

Protection is a system.

An entity does not eliminate responsibility for negligence, fraud, or noncompliance. Separate finances, accurate records, proper formalities, insurance, and compliance controls still matter.

ENTITY COMPARISON

Three common starting points

SIMPLE START

Sole Proprietor

No separate legal entity. Minimal formation requirements and full owner control, with the highest personal-liability tradeoff.

  • Income generally reported on Schedule C
  • Minimal startup administration
  • Unlimited personal liability
BALANCED STRUCTURE

Limited Liability Company

A separate legal entity when properly formed and maintained, offering flexible taxation and manageable formalities.

  • Liability protection for certain business risks
  • Flexible federal tax classification
  • Separate banking and records required
FORMAL SCALE

Corporation

A shareholder-owned legal entity with structured governance, stronger scaling potential, and greater administrative requirements.

  • Strong liability protection
  • C corporation or eligible S corporation treatment
  • Formal governance and recordkeeping
Tax classification and legal entity are related, but not always identical.

For example, an LLC may retain its state-law entity status while electing a different federal tax classification. Obtain advice before filing an election.
