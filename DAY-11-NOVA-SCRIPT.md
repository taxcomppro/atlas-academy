# Day 11 - Nova Production Script

**Lesson:** Choose the Right Location
**Presenter:** Nova Grant, Launch Commander
**Format:** 16:9 landscape, professional and conversational
**Target length:** 3-5 minutes
**Narration:** 421 words; approximately 3.1 minutes at 135 words per minute
**Toolkit source:** pages 38-41

## Narration script

### Scene 1 - Mission hook

A location is not successful simply because it is available or close to home.

### Scene 2 - What to do

Begin with your target clients. Consider local population, income, employment, languages, business ownership, household profile, and the tax services those clients are likely to need. Then evaluate real access. Visit candidate areas during the hours you expect to operate. Observe traffic, turns, parking, public transportation, accessibility, lighting, safety, signs, and the path from arrival to a private meeting.

### Scene 3 - Four operating controls

Use this four-part checklist while you work. Start with target clients. Population, income, employment patterns, language, household profile, and filing needs help define where demand may exist. Observe real access. Visit during operating hours and evaluate traffic, turns, parking, transit, accessibility, safety, lighting, and wayfinding. Map the ecosystem. Complementary businesses can create referrals, while nearby competitors reveal market saturation, service gaps, and pricing expectations. Test infrastructure. Confirm reliable internet, mobile coverage, utilities, privacy, secure document movement, and room for the intended workflow.

### Scene 4 - Build the action plan

Map nearby businesses. Complementary services may produce referrals and convenience. Competitors can reveal demand, saturation, service gaps, and pricing expectations. Finally, test internet reliability, mobile coverage, utilities, privacy, storage, and workflow capacity. Your mission is to score candidate locations with evidence. The best site balances client fit, operating control, visibility, privacy, cost, and room to grow. In your Day 11 action plan, document the following. Target-client fit: Population, income, language, business owners, filing needs. Visibility and traffic: Street exposure, pedestrian flow, signs, and wayfinding. Parking and accessibility: Client entry, transit, mobility, and waiting needs. Competition and referrals: Nearby preparers and complementary businesses. Technology and privacy: Internet, cellular coverage, layout, and confidential flow. Expansion and operating fit: Hours, staffing, storage, growth, and neighborhood safety.

### Scene 5 - Control and completion

As you complete this mission, use a simple three-part control: source, evidence, and owner. Verify the requirement with an official source or a qualified professional. Preserve the document, confirmation, or decision that supports your work. Then assign the next action and due date to a specific person. Do not place Social Security numbers, client return information, account credentials, or other sensitive identifiers in the Academy planner. Before you submit, purchase, sign, or activate anything, separate confirmed facts from assumptions and open questions. Mark every dependency that could delay the next mission. If an item still requires approval or professional review, record it as pending rather than treating it as complete. Include a practical fallback when a vendor, agency, location, or document is not available on schedule. Finish by saving your action plan, recording every professional question, opening both lesson resources, and completing the checkpoint.
