## Narration script

### Scene 1 - Mission hook

Welcome to Day 20. Today your mission is to use due diligence questionnaires. Ask consistently, investigate intelligently, document completely. This mission turns an important launch decision into a documented operating standard that your office can repeat, review, and improve.

### Scene 2 - Why this mission matters

Standardize eligibility questions, follow-up inquiries, supporting evidence, preparer notes, and escalation for high-risk tax benefits.

Structured questionnaires create a reliable starting point for credits, filing status, dependents, self-employment income, and other high-risk areas.

They do not replace judgment. Answers that appear incomplete, inconsistent, or implausible require follow-up questions, supporting evidence, and clear preparer notes before the return advances.

### Scene 3 - Four operating controls

First, focus on coverage. Questions address eligibility, income, dependents, and filing facts. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

Next, focus on follow-up. Prompts require inquiry when answers conflict or seem incomplete. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

Then, focus on evidence. Supporting documents and preparer notes are identified. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

Finally, focus on escalation. Stop, review, and decline rules are clear. Do not mark this area ready based on intention alone. Record the current status, the accountable owner, the evidence that proves completion, and any dependency that could keep the office from moving forward.

### Scene 4 - Build the action plan

Keep this operating rule in view: Never treat a signed questionnaire as proof that an inconsistent answer is correct. When something is uncertain, pause the affected decision, identify the official source or qualified professional who can resolve it, and preserve the answer with the mission records. A deadline, sales claim, or busy-season shortcut is not evidence.

Now complete the Questionnaire Readiness Lab. Work through all four mission areas, name the accountable owner, and identify the document, test, approval, or other evidence that will prove each item is ready. Use the action plan to expose gaps early, while they can still be corrected without disrupting a client or filing deadline.

Your professional-review prompt is this: Choose one high-risk benefit and write the follow-up question you will ask when the first answer is incomplete. Write the answer in plain language. List the facts that support it, the questions that remain open, and the date or event that will trigger another review. Do not place Social Security numbers, client return information, account credentials, or other sensitive identifiers in the Academy worksheet.

### Scene 5 - Control and completion

Before you complete the checkpoint, review the mission from the client's point of view and the office owner's point of view. Ask whether the process is clear, secure, compliant, financially sustainable, and understandable to another trained team member. If the process depends on one person's memory, convert it into a written step, assigned control, or retained record.

When the action plan and checkpoint are complete, continue to Create Staff Accountability Forms. Bring the records from this mission forward so the next decision builds on verified work instead of starting over. I will meet you at the next mission.
